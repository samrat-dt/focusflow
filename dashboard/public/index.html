How Focusflow works

You hand work to Claude sessions and step in only where you are needed. Sending a message never opens a terminal; you open one when you want to watch.

Stages

Backlog
Ideas, parked under their project.
Next
The queue: what you will pick up.
In progress
A session is on it.
Paused
Set aside on purpose; a running session is told to wrap up.
Review
Finished and waiting for you. Approve to close it.
Done
Approved. Notes go to the knowledge base.

Markers

⌨ Open in terminal
A terminal you opened is attached to the task.
⚙ Headless run
Claude is working in the background. Open the task to see what it is doing, or cancel it.
Memory
Everything you tell a task is remembered and given to every future session. Pin it, apply it to the whole project, or forget it from the task's Memory tab.
Later…
Snooze anything on Needs you; it comes back on time, or as soon as something new happens.
! W D
Stale (no activity), Waiting on someone, Due or overdue.
